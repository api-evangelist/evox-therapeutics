Oxford, U.K. April 29, 2024 – Evox Therapeutics Ltd (‘Evox’ or the ‘Company’), a leading exosome therapeutics company, announces that Dr. Per Lundin, Co-Founder and Chief Business Officer, has been appointed as Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of the Company and will be appointed to its Board of Directors.
